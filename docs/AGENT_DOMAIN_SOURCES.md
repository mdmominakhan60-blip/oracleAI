# Agent domain methodology and sources

This document records the epistemic boundaries used by the four specialist profiles. The goal is to make the agents more methodical and useful, not to present divination as validated science.

## Astrology

Urania treats calculated chart positions as computational outputs for the supplied date, time and place. The symbolic meaning attached to those positions belongs to an historical tradition. Birth-time precision controls which claims are allowed: date-only data cannot support houses, Ascendant, MC or exact angles. Transits and synastry are reflective timing and relationship frameworks, not guarantees or access to another person's mind.

## Tarot and Rider-Waite-Smith

The Victoria and Albert Museum describes tarot as a 78-card system with 22 Major Arcana and 56 Minor Arcana and records its origins as a Renaissance Italian card game. It also records the influence of the Rider-Waite-Smith deck, first published in 1909, and Pamela Colman Smith's fully illustrated Minor Arcana. Lenormand therefore preserves card identity, orientation, position, suit and visual evidence before interpretation.

## Palmistry

Mira treats palmistry as a historical symbolic vocabulary applied to visible image evidence. A 2020 article in the *Journal of Neurosciences in Rural Practice*, indexed by PubMed Central, reports a small blinded attempt to compare cheiromantic readings with ALS outcomes and describes the need for further validation. It also distinguishes palmistry from dermatoglyphics. The agent consequently blocks medical, lifespan, pregnancy, death and disease claims and requires image quality and confidence gates.

## Anti-Barnum standard

A professional response must identify its evidence, distinguish observation from interpretation, state uncertainty, provide an alternative explanation and return agency to the user. Generic personality claims, literal predictions, third-party mind reading, medical diagnosis and financial/legal guarantees fail this standard.
